## Preliminary concept

The applicant is exploring whether a phased ATMP/OSAT operation could be technically, commercially, environmentally, and financially feasible in Bangladesh. Potential areas for study include packaging and test services, local workforce development, utility and logistics requirements, supplier availability, and possible collaboration with domestic and international organizations.

No production volume, opening date, export level, job count, local customer demand, project cost, or return on investment is asserted as established. Any figures in subsequent materials will be identified as sourced facts, preliminary estimates, or scenarios and supported by dated evidence and stated assumptions.

## Guidance requested

We would appreciate direction on:

1. The current BIDA process and documentation for an initial investment-facilitation or feasibility discussion.
2. Relevant entity-registration, investment, land, industrial-zone, and licensing requirements, including which authorities are responsible.
3. The appropriate process for evaluating site availability, electricity quality and capacity, water supply, wastewater treatment, transport, customs, and other infrastructure dependencies.
4. Applicable environmental, occupational-safety, building, fire, and industrial permitting reviews and their responsible agencies.
5. Current, authoritative eligibility criteria and application routes for any incentives or public support that might be relevant. This inquiry does not presume eligibility or availability.
6. Workforce and technical-training information, including any established industry or educational coordination channels.
7. The evidence BIDA would expect before considering a more detailed feasibility proposal, pilot plan, or formal investment application.
