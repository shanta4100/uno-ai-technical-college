# Draft Memorandum of Understanding

## Exploratory Clinical AI Workflow Scoping

**Status:** Discussion draft only; not approved, signed, or evidence of STS Group participation  
**Effective date:** To be agreed  
**Term:** To be agreed  
**Important:** This template requires review by qualified counsel in each relevant jurisdiction and approval by each party before signature. It does not authorize access to data, clinical systems, patients, or clinical workflows.

### Parties

1. **GNAIAAAC LLC** (“Developer”), an artificial intelligence, automation, and semiconductor systems development company. Legal name, jurisdiction, registration, address, and signatory authority must be verified.
2. **STS Group or the relevant legal entity** (“Clinical Partner”). The correct entity, authority to sign, address, and signatory must be confirmed by STS Group.

Each may be referred to as a “Party” and together as the “Parties.” Naming STS Group in this draft does not indicate that it has reviewed, accepted, or agreed to participate.

## 1. Purpose

The Parties may explore whether a limited, human-governed clinical AI workflow study is feasible. Any actual pilot would require a separate written protocol and definitive agreement, approved resources, institutional authorization, ethical review where applicable, regulatory review, and data-protection review before any data or clinical workflow is accessed.

This healthcare workstream is separate from any semiconductor, ATMP/OSAT, industrial automation, or investment proposal. It does not validate semiconductor manufacturing capability or establish clinical performance.

## 2. Potential scope for discussion

Subject to separate approval, the Parties may consider:

- A non-production sandbox for evaluation using synthetic data or approved anonymized/de-identified operational and clinical datasets.
- A feasibility assessment of narrowly defined AI-assisted workflow or image-analysis tasks.
- Clinician and technical-staff training on system limitations, review procedures, and escalation.
- A written evaluation plan with intended use, exclusion criteria, acceptance measures, and stop conditions.

No patient care, diagnosis, treatment recommendation, data transfer, system integration, or clinical use is authorized by this MoU alone.

## 3. Clinical governance and safety

- All AI-generated outputs are advisory only and shall be reviewed by qualified healthcare professionals prior to any clinical use.
- The system shall not independently diagnose, triage, prescribe, or determine treatment.
- Any evaluation must define intended use, prohibited use, human review, escalation, incident reporting, monitoring, and emergency suspension procedures.
- No clinical use may begin until the relevant institution and authorities have completed applicable approvals and the Parties have signed a definitive protocol.
- The Developer shall disclose known limitations, evaluation results, model version, data requirements, and material changes relevant to the approved scope.

## 4. Data protection and security

- No data will be accessed, copied, transferred, retained, or processed under this MoU.
- Any later data use must be limited to approved, anonymized or de-identified operational and clinical datasets, subject to applicable approvals, law, institutional policy, and a separate data-processing/data-use agreement.
- The Parties must establish data roles, permitted purposes, access controls, storage location, retention and deletion, breach response, audit rights, and restrictions on onward transfer before data access.
- Re-identification, unauthorized secondary use, model training outside the approved purpose, and disclosure of identifiable information are prohibited unless separately authorized in writing and legally permitted.
- The Parties shall obtain jurisdiction-specific advice regarding current privacy, cybersecurity, health, medical-device, research, and records requirements. This draft does not assert compliance with HIPAA or any specific statute.

## 5. Intellectual property

- Each Party retains its pre-existing intellectual property and confidential know-how.
- Clinical Partner data and clinical records remain subject to the rights and obligations of their lawful owners, custodians, and applicable agreements; this MoU does not transfer ownership.
- No license is granted except one expressly agreed in a later signed instrument.
- Ownership, licensing, publication, and use of any jointly developed work or evaluation results must be set out in a separate written agreement before the work begins.

## 6. Confidentiality and communications

The Parties may agree to a separate confidentiality agreement before exchanging confidential information. Neither Party may use the other’s name, marks, logo, or the existence of discussions in publicity, investor materials, or government submissions without prior written consent.

## 7. Costs and resources

Each Party bears its own discussion costs unless a later signed agreement states otherwise. No funding, equipment, personnel, data, facility access, or reimbursement is committed by this MoU.

## 8. Non-binding effect

This MoU records the Parties’ present intention to explore discussions and is non-binding. No Party is required to proceed with a pilot, commercial transaction, procurement, data-sharing arrangement, or other collaboration. No binding confidentiality, intellectual-property, data-protection, or other obligation is created unless the relevant provision is expressly identified as binding and signed by authorized representatives or is set out in a separate executed agreement. Counsel must adapt this clause to the governing law and the Parties’ intent.

## 9. Term, termination, and dispute handling

The proposed term, governing law, notice method, and dispute process are **to be agreed after legal review**. Either Party may end discussions at any time by written notice. No termination creates an obligation to reimburse costs or continue negotiations.

## 10. Signatures

Signatures are not requested until the Parties verify their legal identities, authority, and approved final text.
