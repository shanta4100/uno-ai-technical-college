Operational events connect to accounting through controls

These flows describe proposed requirements. Actual accounting treatment and tolerances depend on applicable policy, standards, jurisdiction, and entity facts.

3 · Transaction processingCapture → verify → validate → classify → approve → propose/post → reconcile → preserve evidence. Unbalanced or unauthorized entries should be rejected and retained as reviewable exceptions.
4 · General ledgerJournal header and lines → validation (debits = credits) → posting → trial balance and statements. Reconcile control accounts to their subledgers.
5 · Procurement & APRequest → approval → vendor → purchase order → receipt → invoice match → payment workflow → ledger → audit. Vendor approval, invoice validity, duplicate holds, and independent bank-change verification are proposed controls.

Proposed governance mechanics

Resolve rules. Preserve lineage.

Accounting and tax treatments require context and current authoritative sources; a general framework cannot determine the correct treatment for a particular entity or transaction.

Accounting policy context

A proposed policy registry would identify entity, jurisdiction, reporting period, reporting basis, effective date, source authority, and policy version for review. Rules require validation before use; this page is not accounting advice.

Tax rule context

Tax workflows should account for jurisdiction, entity, tax type, effective date, current authority, amendments, and guidance. Simulations and calculations are not tax filings or authority determinations.

Evidence lineage

Proposed traceability: report → statement → account → journal → subledger → business event → source document → authorization. A hash or signature alone does not establish completeness, correctness, or trusted provenance.

Continuous accounting concept

Event → validate → classify → propose journal → reconcile → route exceptions. Detection does not authorize a change; corrections should preserve prior records and their review history.

AML/CFT and audit support

Research concepts include monitoring, evidence, investigation support, and authorized review. An exception is not proof of fraud, and AI analysis is not an audit opinion or an enforcement decision.

Cryptography and digital assets

Cryptography may support authentication and integrity without implying cryptocurrency use. Digital-asset protocol, accounting, tax, and legal treatment are separate questions.
