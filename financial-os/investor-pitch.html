02 / 14 · The problem

Fragmented operations

Organizations coordinate work across disconnected tools.

  • Customer and vendor records
  • Governance and policy documents
  • Security and compliance planning
  • Customer support and workflows
  • Reporting and business administration

Problem hypothesis to validate

Tool sprawl may increase operating cost, coordination complexity, and control gaps. The scale and urgency of these problems—and willingness to pay—must be tested with target customers.

No customer research, quantified pain study, or market-sizing evidence is established in this repository.

09 / 14 · Security & compliance

SOVEREIGN SHIELD · future engineering

Govern, identify, protect, detect, respond, and recover—then verify.

  • Govern: risk registers, ownership, and policies
  • Identify: asset inventories and data classification
  • Protect: MFA, passkeys, RBAC, and encryption planning
  • Detect: logging, monitoring, and audit evidence
  • Incident response, backup, and recovery

Current prototype

Planning fields and draft text only. No MFA, passkeys, biometrics, RBAC, audit logging, encryption controls, monitoring, identity verification, or compliance certification are active.

Security, privacy, and regulatory requirements need qualified review and implementation evidence.
