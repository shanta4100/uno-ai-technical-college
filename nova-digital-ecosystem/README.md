## Executive overview

NOVA Digital Ecosystem is positioned as a configurable enterprise software portfolio for business operations, governance, security planning, compliance workflows, financial administration, analytics, and AI-assisted software generation across potential B2B, B2C, and B2G markets.

The supplied v3.0 positioning treats NOVA Enterprise Suite, Sovereign Shield, NOVA ECON, and SS6CONNECT as separate modules under a common governance and integration framework. GAIOS is described in other supplied material as a three-layer architecture, while other summaries place it at the parent level or omit it from the top-level product families. **Resolve this portfolio hierarchy before external investor or customer distribution.** VEGA NOVA is a separate entertainment/demo concept, not an enterprise-platform module or integration demonstrated here.

## Proposed platform families

### NOVA Enterprise Suite / NOVA CORE

**Purpose:** Customer-configurable organization and business-workspace planning.

Supplied v1.1/v2.1 scope includes company profiles, leadership and departments, governance and compliance records, B2B/B2C/B2G planning, customer and vendor workspaces, CRM, portals, reporting, support planning, document and policy starters, architecture notes, and handover/export concepts.

The repository contains a narrower offline demo at [`/nova-suite/`](../nova-suite/): organization and leadership fields, department and business-model choices, security and provider-readiness planning fields, browser-local profile save/delete, JSON import/export, and three draft policy starters. This does **not** implement a CRM backend, customer portals, a website builder, generated policy workspace, workflow automation, multi-user workspaces, authentication, tenant isolation, or handover-report generator.

### SOVEREIGN SHIELD

**Purpose:** Security, governance, and compliance planning.

Proposed topics include:

- **Govern:** ownership, policies, risk registers, and governance records.
- **Identify:** asset and data inventories.
- **Protect:** MFA, passkey, RBAC, encryption, and access-management planning.
- **Detect:** logging, monitoring, alerts, and audit-evidence planning.
- **Respond:** incident-response and escalation planning.
- **Recover:** backup, continuity, and recovery planning.

These are planning objectives, not active controls or independently verified security practices. The demo does not provide MFA, passkeys, biometrics, RBAC, SSO, session controls, encryption controls, audit logging, monitoring, identity verification, or compliance certification. References to NIST-style categories are conceptual; no framework assessment or conformity has been performed.

### NOVA ECON

**Purpose:** Financial administration and subscription planning.

Proposed work includes pricing, subscription and revenue planning, invoicing/refund-policy drafts, reporting, forecasting, and provider readiness. Catalog examples in supplied materials include Stripe, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Amazon Pay, Adyen, Global Payments, ACH, SEPA, bank transfers, and provider-supported BNPL.

No payment, banking, accounting, cryptocurrency, or merchant integration is connected in this repository. Provider names are examples only; use requires customer accounts, provider approval, contracts, onboarding, fees, and applicable reviews. No transactions occur.

### SS6CONNECT

**Purpose:** AI-assisted software-component and workflow generation (proposed).

Supplied materials describe application, API, dashboard, workflow, template, reporting, deployment-preparation, monitoring-template, and documentation generation. They also describe an enterprise control plane for applications, users, billing, content, deployments, analytics, support, and security. These capabilities, control-plane services, and AI outputs are not implemented or demonstrated in this repository.

SS6CONNECT is not a bank, payment network, government authority, telecom carrier, settlement system, or regulated service provider. Regulated activities require appropriately authorized third parties and customer approvals. AI recommendations require human review and organizational accountability.

### GAIOS architecture concept

Some supplied materials describe GAIOS as an architecture backbone:

1. **Facts and data ingestion:** potential public sources such as IMF, World Bank, BEA, BLS, Census, and central-bank publications.
2. **Analytics and intelligence:** proposed survey analysis, statistical methods, forecasting, NLP, knowledge graphs, and business intelligence.
3. **Applications:** proposed APIs, search, portals, learning, commerce, and administrative tools.

The design principle is to keep source facts and provenance distinct from AI interpretation. No source-data connectors, verified-data pipeline, analytics engine, knowledge graph, data-lineage system, or GAIOS implementation is established here. This architecture is a concept, not a claim of verified datasets or results.

## Customer, deployment, and integration boundaries

The customer-control model is a **design goal**, not a verified production guarantee. Proposed customer control includes branding, domains, DNS, hosting, databases, accounts, recovery, security settings, providers, integrations, and exported data. Approved, logged, time-limited, revocable support access and no undisclosed access are requirements to design, implement, and independently test.

The current prototype stores profile data in this browser's `localStorage`. It is not encrypted, authenticated, synchronized, backed up, or isolated between users of the same browser profile. JSON export is portability only. Do not enter sensitive, confidential, identity, financial-account, or government information.

No legal entity or government account is created; no regulatory document is filed; no compliance certification, identity, or license is verified; no payment is processed; no email/SMS/call is transmitted; and no external service is connected. Satellite/telecommunications items are planning placeholders only.

## AI governance and risk principles

Proposed principles are human review, traceable sources, explainable outputs, auditability, least privilege, clear accountability, and evidence-based rollout. They are design targets, not assurances of platform behavior today. Avoid claims of autonomous authority, automatic compliance, guaranteed cybersecurity, or guaranteed financial results.
