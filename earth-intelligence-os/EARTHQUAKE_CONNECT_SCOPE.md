# Earthquake Connect — Scientific Scope and Exclusions

**Status:** Proposed research and data-integration scope; operational status requires independent verification.

## Intended scope

Earthquake Connect may organize attributed earthquake observations, support scientific analysis, and present clearly labeled research findings or simulations. Every observation must preserve its provider/source attribution and the provider’s event timestamp separately from retrieval or ingestion time. Corrections and derived interpretations should retain traceable links to the source records.

Use evidence labels consistently:

- **Provider observation:** A record published by an identified source; label it as provider-reported, not independently verified.
- **Verified finding:** A result checked against documented evidence and a stated verification method.
- **Estimate:** A derived value with method, assumptions, units, uncertainty, and version.
- **Research:** An analysis not validated for operational use.
- **Simulation:** Synthetic or modeled output that is visibly distinguished from real observations.

Do not turn a provider observation into a “verified finding” merely by ingesting it. Preserve source identity, event time, retrieval time, units, quality flags, and correction history where available.

## Explicit exclusions

- **Experimental quantum research:** No physical quantum experiments or quantum sensing are included. Computational models do not establish experimental validation.
- **Physical infrastructure control:** No control of buildings, utilities, satellites, machinery, or emergency systems is included.
- **Autonomous decision publication:** Research outputs require human review before publication as institutional decisions or public advisories. The product is not an official warning or emergency-response authority.
- **Financial transaction processing:** No payment execution, fund transfers, custody, trading, or banking operations are included.

## Evidence and readiness requirements

Claims of earthquake prediction, early warning, calibrated probability, or operational readiness require separate, independently reviewed validation. Such validation must define the target and time horizon, ground truth, evaluation period, sample size, baselines, uncertainty, false-alarm and missed-event rates, and failure cases. Do not use simulations or retrospective fit as proof of prospective predictive performance.

No claim of a live provider connection, validated forecast, active warning service, physical sensing, or operational deployment is established by this scope document. The existing Earth Intelligence dashboard is an offline concept prototype; use official providers and emergency-management authorities for safety decisions.
