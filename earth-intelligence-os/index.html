Guardian Index & Earth Intelligence Kernel

GI = 100 × (P × C × T × Q)1/4

P = probability · C = confidence · T = validation trust · Q = data quality. Inputs, calibration, missing-data rules, and index meaning require formal research and validation.

EIK = αS + βG + γW + δM + εO + ζH

S = seismic · G = GPS · W = weather · M = geomagnetic · O = ocean · H = historical. Weights and outputs are not established; no calculation is performed here.
